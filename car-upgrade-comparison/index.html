The wider search

Why some names remain secondary

01

Prove the comfort upgrade

Allow a 45–60 minute test drive. Try rough roads, motorway noise, lumbar and thigh support. The Mach-E and pre-Juniper Model Y need particular scrutiny.

02

Bring the real luggage

Load the pushchair and cases with child seats fitted. The Mach-E is close in total storage but its 402 L rear boot + frunk is less flexible for one bulky item.

03

Buy the right example

Check full service history, completed recalls, warranty terms, tyres and brakes. For EVs, request battery health and test charging. Get an insurance quote by registration.

How to read this comparison. Priorities are editorial judgements about this brief, not reliability scores. “Latest major design” is assessed on the research date. Early-2023 registrations may already be over three years old. Prices and equipment must be reconfirmed before purchase.
