What counts as evidence?

Numeric-derived: price, boot capacity, estimated real range, DC charging time, mi/kWh, reliability percentage and warranty calculations. Editorial: ride, seats, cabin, driver assistance, rear-seat spaciousness, sourcing difficulty and operating costs. Missing: a neutral 6/10 reliability prior where no verified comparable model-level survey exists. These are not laboratory measurements or statistical reliability probabilities.

Private local configuration

Selected cars, scoring weights, per-car changes and eligibility overrides are stored in this browser only. Use Export to move the configuration to another device. We do not write your preferences back into the public spreadsheet.

How knockout works

All eligible selected entrants are shuffled with a deterministic seeded generator. A conventional elimination bracket adds random first-round byes to reach a power of two. In each pairing, the higher unrounded weighted score wins. Equal results use stable car ID alphabetical order as a tie-breaker. For one entrant, it is declared champion without a match. A knockout bracket is an illustration of the path: with fixed ratings its highest-ranked entrant will win regardless of random pairings.
