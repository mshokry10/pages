Recommended

Loki — Season 2
Series · 2023
MustLean route

The Marvels
Movie · 2023
Strongly useful

Its ending contains meaningful X-Men/multiverse setup. You can understand the movie without watching all of Ms. Marvel.

Do I need Ms. Marvel first?Optional
No. Ms. Marvel gives Kamala Khan a fuller introduction, but it is not important enough to the Doomsday path to make six extra episodes mandatory. Watch it only if you want more Kamala context before The Marvels.

For this focused route, you can leave X-Men Origins: Wolverine, First Class, The Wolverine, Days of Future Past, Apocalypse, Dark Phoenix and New Mutants for later. They add lore, but they are not required to follow the core route above.
