What “term-time” means: the universal entitlement is normally 15 hours × 38 weeks = 570 hours/year. Oxley Park and Ducklings broadly follow the school calendar, so they close for half-terms, Christmas, Easter and the summer break. Acorn operates for about 49 weeks, or roughly 11 more weeks of availability per year. The difference is about childcare coverage, not simply the weekly fee.

How to compare the costs

The monthly figures are averaged over 12 months. The effective hourly figure is the amount you pay after funding divided by all childcare hours booked under the assumed schedule, including the 15 funded hours. This makes the annual totals easier to compare on a per-hour basis, but the nurseries still provide different annual coverage: Acorn is available for roughly 11 more weeks than the term-time settings.

*Acorn's figures remain planning estimates because its exact funded-hours allocation and afternoon-session length need to be confirmed directly. Funding arrangements and provider fees can change, so the final comparison should use written quotes from each setting.
